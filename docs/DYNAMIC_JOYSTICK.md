# Dynamic joystick

Enable Options → Dynamic joystick (left side), then launch the game. Default is off.

A new touch anywhere in the left half, outside action buttons, places the analog joystick center at that touch. It starts neutral, keeps its center during dragging, and disappears when released. A second finger can use action buttons. Existing fingers are never recaptured when the joystick finger lifts. A new touch creates a new center. The fixed position and custom size are preserved; arrow D-pad mode stays fixed.

Device checks: toggle and relaunch; touch two different left-side positions; drag beyond the radius; release; verify neutral/no idle joystick; hold Jump while moving; lift the movement finger while another finger remains; check fixed mode and arrow mode; check transparency and saved preference after restart.
